## Description (paste into Kaggle)
This is a free SAMPLE — the first 500 records of **NYC Motor Vehicle Collisions — 50,000 Most Recent Crashes (Dataset + Report)**, published by Planet Leads 🚀.

The data is derived from NYC Open Data: the "Motor Vehicle Collisions — Crashes" dataset (Socrata ID h9gi-nx95), published by the New York City Police Department (NYPD). The full source holds 2,304,731 crashes; the full product contains the 50,000 most recent crashes (2026-04-10 to 2026-11-08), ordered newest first. This sample is the first 500 of those 50,000. No rows were edited, estimated, or invented, and no personal data is included.

**The full product ($19 on Gumroad)** contains all 50,000 records as CSV, a PDF summary report, and the full data dictionary:
https://rayhymn.gumroad.com/l/fufftx

What you are paying for there is the cleaning, packaging, documentation, and snapshot — the underlying public data remains the City of New York's. NYC Open Data carries no restrictions on use (Local Law 11 of 2012); attribution to the City of New York / NYPD is included here as a courtesy and as the product's own practice.

Columns: crash_date, crash_time, borough, zip_code, latitude, longitude, on_street_name, cross_street_name, off_street_name, injury/killed counts for persons, pedestrians, cyclists, and motorists, contributing_factor_vehicle_1/2, vehicle_type_code1/2. See `data_dictionary.md` for full definitions and honest notes (contributing factors are police judgments at the scene, not court findings).
