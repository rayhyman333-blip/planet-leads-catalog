## Description (paste into Kaggle)
This is a free SAMPLE — 150 of the 428 products in **Planet Leads Price Trends — Real Store Prices, Tracked Daily**, published by Planet Leads 🚀.

The full product is a dated snapshot of real list prices from the Planet Leads Shopper storefront, taken 2026-11-11: 428 products across 16 categories (Books, Organizers, Courses, Clothing, Digital Products, and more), prices from $4.00 to $149.00, median $16.99. Columns: product_id, title, category, price_usd (the lowest variant price), date_snapshot, source, vendor. No prices were edited, estimated, or invented.

**The full product ($19 on Gumroad)** contains the complete 428-product CSV, a 3-page PDF report (category breakdown, 10 highest- and 10 lowest-priced products, collection method), and the price tracker script that records the same store's prices daily so buyers can build their own trend history:
https://rayhymn.gumroad.com/l/csbsbp

Honest limits, stated in the product itself: one snapshot cannot show a trend and nothing here predicts what will sell; the file contains list prices only — no supplier costs, margins, or sales data.
