## Description (paste into Kaggle)
This is a free SAMPLE — the first 500 records of **NYC Restaurant Inspections — 50,000 Recent Inspections (Dataset + Report)**, published by Planet Leads 🚀.

The data is derived from NYC Open Data: the DOHMH New York City Restaurant Inspection Results dataset (Socrata ID 43nn-pn8j), published by the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene. The full source holds 294,700 records; the full product contains the 50,000 most recent inspections (2026-05-21 to 2026-11-09), ordered newest first. This sample is the first 500 of those 50,000. No rows were edited, estimated, or invented.

**The full product ($19 on Gumroad)** contains all 50,000 records as CSV, a PDF summary report, and the full data dictionary:
https://rayhymn.gumroad.com/l/dxzqdc

What you are paying for there is the cleaning, packaging, documentation, and snapshot — the underlying public data remains the City of New York's. NYC Open Data carries no restrictions on use (Local Law 11 of 2012); attribution to the City of New York / DOHMH is included here as a courtesy and as the product's own practice.

Columns: camis (restaurant ID), dba (trade name), boro, building, street, zipcode, phone (the business's public listing number), cuisine_description, inspection_date, action, violation_code, violation_description, critical_flag, score, grade, grade_date, record_date. See `data_dictionary.md` for the full definitions and honest notes (e.g., 23,670 of the 50,000 full-product rows carry no letter grade).
