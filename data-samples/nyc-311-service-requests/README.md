## Description (paste into Kaggle)
This is a free SAMPLE — the first 500 records of **NYC 311 Service Requests — 50,000 Most Recent Requests (Dataset + Report)**, published by Planet Leads 🚀.

The data is derived from NYC Open Data: the "311 Service Requests from 2020 to Present" dataset (Socrata ID erm2-nwe9), published by NYC 311. The full source holds 23,100,861 requests; the full product contains the 50,000 most recent requests (2026-11-05 to 2026-11-10 — about five days of citywide volume), ordered newest first. This sample is the first 500 of those 50,000. No rows were edited, estimated, or invented.

**The full product ($19 on Gumroad)** contains all 50,000 records as CSV, a PDF summary report, and the full data dictionary:
https://rayhymn.gumroad.com/l/iwcugm

What you are paying for there is the cleaning, packaging, documentation, and snapshot — the underlying public data remains the City of New York's. NYC Open Data carries no restrictions on use (Local Law 11 of 2012); attribution to the City of New York / NYC 311 is included here as a courtesy and as the product's own practice.

Columns: unique_key, created_date, closed_date, agency, agency_name, complaint_type, descriptor, location_type, incident_zip, incident_address, city, borough, status, community_board, latitude, longitude. See `data_dictionary.md` for full definitions and honest notes (a "Closed" status means the city closed the request; it does not prove the underlying problem was fixed).
